**Nexus Gaja** ye kunnafoniko sira hakilitigi ye, min bɛ sigidakow dɔn, min dabɔra walasa ka diɲɛ kunnafoniko caman sɛmɛntiya.

## Laɲini ni Yeli

![Nexus Gaja Vision](nafolo/img/nexus_vision.jpg) Bamako, Mali.

Diɲɛ kɔnɔ min falen don diɲɛ kɔnɔ, tuma caman na, kan de ye balilanba ye. Nexus Gaja ka laɲini fɔlɔ ye ka kumaɲɔgɔnya kɛ mɔgɔw ni ɲɔgɔn cɛ, min tɛ fɛn tiɲɛ, min bɛ se ka sɔrɔ ani min bɛ bɛn sigida ma - u mana kan kelen fɔ wo, u tɛ kan kelen fɔ wo, o mana kɛ cogo o cogo. 

A tɛ daɲɛw bamanankan na ni kuncɛbaya dɔrɔn ye, nka **kɔrɔ caman cili** de don. Nexus Gaja bɛ mɔgɔw siri ɲɔgɔn na cogo jugu la, a kɛtɔ ka laadalakow, marabolow ani sigidako fɛnɲɛnɛmaw faamuya, ka baro lakikaw, lakikaw kɛ sababu ye.

## Fɛn minnu bɛ se ka kɛ ani minnu bɛ se ka kɛ
- **Multimedia communication**: Sistɛmu tɛ sɛbɛnni dɔrɔn kɛ, nka a bɛ ja, lamɛntaw ani wideyow fana baara. O bɛ kɛ sababu ye ka barow kɛ minnu bɛ mɔgɔ su ji la kosɛbɛ (misali la, wideyow weleli walima kumakan cikanw) waati yɛrɛ la kan dancɛw kɔnɔ.
- **Context sensitivity**: Irony, idioms, jargon ani marabolokanw sɔrɔli minnu bɛ faamuyabaliya tuma caman na laadala bamanankanfalennaw fɛ.
- **Cross-platform network** : A bɛ kɛ basigi ye mɔgɔ kelen-kelen ka kumaɲɔgɔnyaw, forobaciyɛnw (postes ni commentaires) ani diɲɛ sigidamɔgɔw ka jɛɲɔgɔnyaw.

## Tekniki architecture (hakilila jɔnjɔn) .

![Nexus Gaja bamanankan bamanankan hakilina](nafolo/img/nexus_translation.jpg)

Nexus Gaja ka fɛɛrɛbɔ-yɔrɔ ye kumaɲɔgɔnya misali ye min bɛ a yɛrɛ labɛn, min tilalen don kosɛbɛ ka kɛ layini saba ye:

1. **Original**: Kumaɲɔgɔnya fɛn (cikan) min dabɔra ciden fɛ, o bɛ to a cogo la tuma bɛɛ.
2. **Kɔrɔfɔli**: Sistɛmu tɛ daɲɛw dɔrɔn sɛgɛsɛgɛ, nka kɔrɔ yɛrɛ fana sɛgɛsɛgɛ.
3. **kan laɲinitaw jiracogo**: AI bɛ fɔlɔ ta jiracogo waatilama walima a maralen dɔrɔn de dabɔ a sɔrɔbaga kelen-kelen bɛɛ ye ka da u ka kan fɛ min ka di u ye. Bamanankan baarakɛcogo tɛ cikan fɔlɔ sɛbɛn abada.

### Contexte (kofɔlen) ka jɔyɔrɔfalen
Bamanankan baarakɛcogo tɛ kibaruyaw lajɛ abada Nexus Gaja kɔnɔ u dan na. Motɛri bɛ jateminɛ kɛ jirisunw bɛɛ lajɛlen na:
`Cikan` → `Cikan tɛmɛnenw` → `Fɛɛrɛw kɔnɔko` → `Sigida kɔnɔko` → `Kan / Mara` → `Baarakɛlaw ka fɛɛrɛw`

### Nafa sɔrɔli baarakɛcogo bamanankan baarakɛcogo la min bɛ kɛ ka kɛɲɛ ni ɲinini ye
Bamanankan bamanankan bɛ Kɛ dɔrɔn **ni a Weelela** (ni ɲinini kɛra) nafolo maracogo la. Ni baarakɛla dɔ ye kɔnɔkow ɲini, u bɛ baara kɛ n’u ka kan kɔrɔ ye. Kan kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen dɔ bamanankan-falen bɛ mara kudayi (caching) walasa ka ɲininkaliw teliya kosɛbɛ kɔfɛ.

## AI-powered moderation (WP 1.8.4) .

![Nexus Gaja AI Moderation](nafolo/img/nexus_moderation.jpg) Bamako, Mali.

Ni AI dɛmɛna ni danbe ye, an bɛ senba ta ka bɔ fɛn dilannenw hakilina na ka taa fɛɛrɛbɔcogo la, ka jateminɛ kɛ UE ka sariyaw la minnu bɛ sen na sisan (UE ka AI sariya ka jɛlenya wajibiyalenw ka kɛɲɛ ni sariyasen 50nan ye; Nizɛri baarakɛminɛnw sariya ni dalilu faamuyalenw ni sɔsɔli suganditaw).

### 1. Sariyakolo jɔnjɔn
Kumasen min nafa ka bon kosɛbɛ fɛn dilanni na, o ye : **Moderation AI ye sɛgɛsɛgɛli kɛcogo ye, a tɛ yɛrɛmahɔrɔnya sariyasun ye.**
A laɲini ye ka mɔgɔw dɛmɛ ni danma ye, a ma kɛ k’a dɔn hakilina minnu bɛ sɔn ka kɛ Nexus Gaja kan.
O cogo la, an bɛ danfara don fɛn saba ni ɲɔgɔn cɛ:
- **Detect:** “Sariya tiɲɛni bɛ se ka kɛ yan.”
- **Hakɛ:** “Misali la, sariya tiɲɛni seko ye 94% ye.”
- **A latigɛ:** “Fɛn jumɛn bɛna kɛ tiɲɛ na?”
Nivo sabanan ka kan ka kɔlɔsi hadamadenya siratigɛ la ko juguw la.

### 2. Moderasiyɔn AI bɛna kɛ a yɛrɛ ka subsystem ye
Sani AI kelen ka kɛ, sigida fitinin barikama dɔ bɛ sigi sen kan:
```sɛbɛnni
                 NEXUS GAJA AI ƝƐNABƆLI
                          │
       ┌────────────────── ┼──────────────────┐
       │ │ │
  Kan AI Lakanali AI nanbara AI
       │ │ │
       ├─────────────────── ┴──────────────────┤
       │ │ │
 Bamanankan baarakɛcogo dɔnniya
 Sɛgɛsɛgɛli Sɛgɛsɛgɛli Siginidenw
       │ │ │
       └────────────────────────────────────┘
                      ▼
               Faratiw jateminɛni
                      │
                      ▼
               Hadamadenw ka Seginkanni
```

### 3. AI modulu minnu nafa ka bon kosɛbɛ
Sɛgɛsɛgɛli yɔrɔ kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen 9 bɛ Nexus Gaja la:
- **M1 – Kan faamuyali** : A bɛ kan, kanhakɛ, slang, ironi jiralanw, bamanankan gɛlɛyaw kɔlɔsi.
- **M2 – Toxicité / Abuse Detection**: A bɛ mafiɲɛyali, mɔgɔ yɛrɛ binkanniw, tɔɔrɔw dɔn.
- **M3 – Bagabagali dɔnni**: A bɛ bagabagali, bagabagali, ani bagabagali minnu bɛ se ka kɛ fariyakow la, olu dɔn.
- **M4 – Hate / Dehumanization Detection**: A bɛ binkanniw kɔlɔsi minnu ɲɛsinnen bɛ mɔgɔw ma ka da u ni ɲɔgɔn cɛ jɛɲɔgɔnya kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnenw kan.
- **M5 – Spam / Manipulation Detection**: A bɛ spam, bot kɛcogo, manipule coordinated kɔlɔsi.
- **M6 – Nanbarako dɔnni**: A bɛ nanbarako ɲininiw dɔn minnu bɛ mɔgɔ sikɛ, phishing, social engineering.
- **M7 – Identity Integrity**: A bɛ taamasiyɛnw lajɛ minnu ɲɛsinnen bɛ jatebɔsɛbɛnw tali ma, jatebɔsɛbɛn caman, bloki bypass.
- **M8 – Media Safety**: A bɛ ja, lamɛntaw, wideyow, sɛbɛnw sɛgɛsɛgɛ.
- **M9 – Context Engine**: Module min nafa ka bon kosɛbɛ. A bɛ mɔgɔ kelen-kelen ka sɔrɔw lajɛ ɲɔgɔn fɛ.

### 4. Mun na Context Engine nafa ka bon kosɛbɛ
Daɲɛ kolomaw ɲinini saniyalen tɛna se ka kɛ. “Ne tun bɛ se k’a faga ni nisɔndiya ye” kɔrɔko fariyakow bɛ yen, nka a ye kumasen ye. « Sini nɛgɛ kanɲɛ 8 waati n bɛna marifa ci a la a ka so ɲɛfɛ » o ye ko wɛrɛ ye pewu. AI ka kan k’a faamu ko kumasen kɔrɔ ye min ye nin ko kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen in na.

### 5. Kan caman fɔcogo
Damatɛmɛni tɛ se ka daɲɛw suma ɲɔgɔn na dɔrɔn. A ka kan ka kɔrɔ hakɛ sɛgɛsɛgɛ (misali la, Alemaɲi kumasenw ni Zapɔnkan fɔcogo ni marabolo fɔcogo).

### 6. Kan fɔlɔ + bamanankan baarakɛcogo
A fɔlɔ ni a bayɛlɛmani bɛ sɛgɛsɛgɛ u danma. O dɔrɔn de la, “Assessment de moderation combinée” bɛ kɛ. O cogo la, Nexus Gaja bɛ se k’a dɔn ni bamanankan baarakɛcogo yɛrɛ ye ko juguya walima k’a Changé.

### 7. Daŋaniya Sɔrɔ
AI jateminɛ kelen-kelen bɛɛ bɛ dannaya nafa sɔrɔ (misali la, Bagabagali sɔrɔli: 0,96). Nka: **Dannaya hakɛ ≠ Tiɲɛ.** Ni jate kɛra 96% ye, o kɔrɔ dɔrɔn ye ko modɛli dalen bɛ a ka danfara la kosɛbɛ, a man kan ko baarakɛla jalakilen don.

### 8. Dankarili yɛrɛ bɛ kɛ taamasyɛn ye
Ni AI ma da a la (misali la, Bagabagali: 0,62, Satire: 0,54), a tɛ se ka wale gɛlɛnw kɛ dɔrɔn. O nɔ na, ​​dannayabaliya bɛ jɔ k’a ɲɛsin fɛn dilanni ma: **Hadamaden ka seginnkanni wajibiyalen don**.

### 9. Laɲini yɔrɔ naani
- 🟢 **GREEN**: A ka c'a la, a bɛ sariya labato. → wale si tɛ kɛ.
- 🟡 **YELLOW**: Dankarili bɛ se ka kɛ. → kɔlɔsili / kɔlɔsili ni a mago bɛ a la.
- 🟠 **ORANGE**: A bɛ se ka kɛ sariya tiɲɛni ye. → Moderasiyɔn sɛgɛsɛgɛli.
- 🔴 **RED**: Dankariliba min bɛ se ka kɛ. → teliya la lakanani fɛɛrɛ + hadamadenw ka sɛgɛsɛgɛli.

### 10. “AI jalaki” tɛ .
**AI tɛ sankɔrɔta labanw kɛ.** A bɛ se ka fɛɛrɛ kɔrɔtɔlenw daminɛ (misali la, cikan minɛni) ni lakana haminankobaw kɛra, nka desizɔn laban bɛ to ka sɛgɛsɛgɛ.

### 11. Lakanali fɛɛrɛw bɛ se ka kɛ otomatiki ye
Ni bagabagali kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnen dɔ bɛ yen (Bagabagali dɔnna → Daŋaniyaba → Dantigɛli waati dɔɔnin → Hadamaden ka seginnkanni → Desizɔn), an bɛ bagabagalikɛla lakana k’a sɔrɔ an ma AI kɛ kiritigɛla ye.

### 12. AI ka kan ka se ka jo di a ka latigɛ ma
DSA bɛ dalilu jɛlenw ni kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnenw de wajibiya. AI ka daliluw bɛ kɛ cogo la min sigilen don : Sariya (NG-CONDUCT-004), A kɔlɔsira (Baara bɛ se ka kɛ min bɛ se ka kɛ), Daŋaniya (0,94), Sigida min bɛ tali kɛ o la (Cikan 4 tɛmɛnenw), Waleya ladilikan (Hadamadenw ka seginnkanni).

### 13. AI man kan ka kɔnɔkow Changer gundo la
**Moderation AI man kan ka kunnafoni fɔlɔw Changer abada k’a sɔrɔ u ma kɔlɔsi.** Otomatiki latilenni, bamanankan bamanankan walima kumasen kuncɛlenw bɛ kɔnɔko fɔlɔ mara.

### 14. AI ye kɔnɔkow labɛn
An bɛ danfara don ninnu ni ɲɔgɔn cɛ: hadamadenw ye minnu da, AI dɛmɛni, AI ye ani AI ye. O bɛ kɛ kɔnɔkow metadata dɔ ye.

### 15. AI kɔnɔkow taamasiyɛnni & AI bɔyɔrɔ layɛrɛ
Ka kɛɲɛ ni UE ka AI sariya ka jɛlenya sariyaw ye (kabini san 2026 awirilikalo la), AI ye kunnafoni minnu dilan, olu ka kan ka kɛ fɛn ye min bɛ dɔn. An bɛ AI Provenance Layer dɔ labɛn min bɛ metadata (AI bɔyɔrɔ, modɛli, waati taamasiyɛn, hadamaden ka seginnkanni) mara.

### 16. Deepfake sɔrɔli
O jɔli dabɔra ka ja sintetikiw, kanw lawulilenw ani deepfakew dɔn. Nka dɔnniya tɛ dalilu ye a yɛrɛma.

### 17. “Tiɲɛ masin” otomatiki tɛ (damatɛmɛ ≠ tiɲɛ sɛgɛsɛgɛli) .
Sistɛmu dɔ b’a lajɛ: “Yala kɔnɔkow bɛ sariya dɔw tiɲɛ wa?” (Content Moderation), dɔ wɛrɛ bɛ nin di: "Kunnafoni ni sɔrɔyɔrɔ jumɛnw bɛ yen?" (Kunnafoni dɛmɛni). Hakilinaw tɛ bɔ dɔrɔn i n’a fɔ “jugu”.

### 18. Lakanali ka bɔ laadalakow faamuyali jugu la
AI b’a ɲini **Cultural Context Models** fɛ walasa ka jamana kelen ka kumaɲɔgɔnya sariya bali ka kɛ diɲɛ sariyasun ye.

### 19. Ironie, satiriki ani nisɔndiya
AI bɛ baara kɛ ni sigida, emojis, baro taabolo ani irony structures dɔntaw ye, nka a ka kan ka sira di dannayabaliya ma ni kɔrɔw ma jɛya.

### 20. Jalaki tɛ min sinsinnen bɛ AI jate kelen kan
Modration intervention jugu si tɛ se ka sinsin otomatiki danfara jaabi kelen dɔrɔn kan (Text + Context + Behavior + Language + Media + Rule Engine = Risk Assessment).

### 21. Baarakɛlaw ka taamacogo taamasiyɛnw & No Social Credit System
A bɛ tali kɛ fɛɛrɛko juguya taamasiɲɛw la (misali la, spam mass posting), a tɛ tali kɛ sosiyete ka jatebɔcogo caman na. Nexus Gaja tɛ sosiyete ka jurudon sira baara - moderation ye lakana de kama wa a tɛ kɛ ka mɔgɔ nafa jateminɛ.

### 22. Moderation AI ka kan ka kɛ auditable ye
Otomatiki latigɛ minnu bɛ tali kɛ o ko la, olu bɛɛ bɛ sɛbɛn (ko kɛlenw ID, sariya ID, dannaya, hadamadenw ka seginnkanni, a ɲɔgɔnnaw) walasa ka tugu ɲɔgɔn kɔ.

### 23. Pozitif nkalonmaw, Negatif nkalonmaw & Jogoɲumanya jateminɛw
Misali suguyaw bɛ kɔlɔsi. Dashboard bɛ sɛgɛsɛgɛli kɛ tigitigi, hakilijigin ani kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnenya la **Appeal Reversal Rate** (appel minnu ye ɲɛtaa sɔrɔ, olu hakɛ). 

### 24. Kanko tilennenya & bamanankan baarakɛcogo bias
Moderasiyɔn jogo ka kan ka se ka suma kan bɛɛ la minnu bɛ dɛmɛ (Multilingual Moderation Benchmark). Ni moderation jaabiw tɛ kelen ye fɔlɔ ni bamanankan bamanankan na (bamanankan bamanankan sɔsɔ), o ka kan ka lajɛ u danma.

### 25. Architecture Proposition & Politiki Motɛri
Sariyasunw (politiki motɛri) ma hard-coded don AI models kɔnɔ. AI bɛ hakilinaw di, politiki motɛri bɛ latigɛw kɛ ka da sariyaw kan minnu bɛ sen na sisan. O bɛ sira Di **model caman caman cili ma k'a sɔrɔ sariyaw ma Changé**.

### 26. Mɔgɔw bɛ to fanga laban ye
- **NG-AI-MOD-001**: AI bɛ dɛmɛ don kɔlɔsili ni danfara la, nka a tɛ hadamaden ka seginnkanni bila a nɔ na ni a bɛ latigɛ gɛlɛnw kɛ.
- **NG-AI-MOD-002**: Otomatiki moderation decisions ka kan ka tugu, ka sɛbɛn ani ka sɛgɛsɛgɛ.

**Kunnafoni**: An bɛ ka sigida naani jɔ : AI dɔnni, sigida ni faratiw sɛgɛsɛgɛli, politiki motɛri ani hadamadenw ka mara. O bɛ kɛ sababu ye ka otomatiki barikama kɛ k’a sɔrɔ a ma “AI as judge” architecture faratilen dɔ da.

## API, interface ani communication architecture (WP 1.11.3) .

Walasa ka sitɛmu sabatili, lakana ani sɛgɛsɛgɛli sabati, Nexus Gaja bɛ tugu API-based ni ko kɛlenw ɲɛnabɔcogo la kosɛbɛ.

### Sariyakolo jɔnjɔnw
- **Dɔnniyasɛbɛnw sɔrɔli siratigɛ la :** Yɔrɔw bɛ kumaɲɔgɔnya kɛ dɔrɔn kunnafonidilanw fɛ minnu ɲɛfɔlen don (API walima ko kɛlenw), abada kunnafonidilan ɲininkali tilennenw fɛ minnu bɛ bɔ baarakɛyɔrɔ wɛrɛw la.
- **API Gateway:** Kɛnɛma kliyanw ka ɲininiw bɛɛ bɛ tɛmɛ API Gateway fɛ min bɛ dantigɛli, sirako ani hakɛ dantigɛli ɲɛnabɔ.
- **Provider abstraction:** Kɛnɛma baarakɛminɛnw (AI modɛliw, sarali dilanbagaw, bamanankan baarakɛcogo masinw) bɛ don ɲɔgɔn na abstraction layers fɛ. O bɛ dantigɛli gɛlɛnw bali ani ka sira di furakɛlikɛlaw ka fɛn caman sɛgɛsɛgɛli ma.

### Kumaɲɔgɔnya kɛcogo
- **Synchronous APIs (REST/HTTPS):** A bɛ kɛ ka teliya ɲininiw kɛ i n’a fɔ doncogo, profil sigicogo walima bamanankan baarakɛcogo tilennen.
- **Ko kɛlenw minnu tɛ ɲɔgɔn sɔrɔ (Ko kɛlenw Bus):** Nexus Gaja ka cɛmancɛ-sɔgɔsɔgɔninjɛ min bɛ kɛ ka ɲɛsin walew ma minnu tɛ ɲɔgɔn ta (misali la `Message.Created` bɛ danbe, bamanankan bamanankan ani kunnafonidi daminɛ asynchrone).
- **Waati yɛrɛ la (WebSocket):** Kanal kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnenw ka ɲɛsin baro ɲɛnamaw ni sɛbɛnni cogoya ma.

### Lakanali ni dannaya
- **Zero Trust Model:** Kɔnɔna rezow taamacogo tɛ dannaya kɛ a yɛrɛma ; baara ni baarakɛyɔrɔ kumaɲɔgɔnya min bɛ kɛ ni hakili ye, o bɛ dantigɛli de wajibiya.
- **Idempotence & Outbox Pattern:** Baarakɛcogo kɔrɔlenw (i n’a fɔ bolomafaraw walima cikanw) dabɔra ka kɛ idempotent ye walasa ka baara caman bali ani ka baara kɛ ni outbox pattern ye walasa ko kɛlenw kana tunun hali kunnafonidilanw ka jagokow la.

## MVP Domɛni Modeli (WP 1.12) .

![Nexus Gaja Modular Monolith](nafolo/img/nexus_architecture.jpg) Bamako, Mali.

Nexus Gaja bɛ baara kɛ ni MVP architecture (ADR-025) ye min bɛ tali kɛ domani na kosɛbɛ, min dilannen don i n’a fɔ monolith modulaire ni domaine dancɛ jɛlenw. O sigicogo in bɛ mikrosɛrɛwisi fɔlɔw ka gɛlɛya bali k’a sɔrɔ a bɛ sɛgɛsɛgɛli sabati walasa ka domani kɛrɛnkɛrɛnnenw bila kɔfɛ.

### Fɛnɲɛnɛmako jɔnjɔnw
O jɔcogo bɛ hakilinaw fara ɲɔgɔn kan kosɛbɛ walasa ka kunnafonidilanw dafalen sabati ani ka filiw bali i n’a fɔ “baarakɛla tɔgɔ = hadamaden”:
- **Identity & Accounts:** `Mɔgɔ` ≠ `Baarakɛla ka jatebɔ` ≠ `Dɔnniya sɛgɛsɛgɛli`. Mɔgɔ min sɛgɛsɛgɛra, o bɛ a sen don a la jatebɔsɛbɛn kelen fɛ, nka jɛkuluw bɛ to u dan na.
- **Kunnafoni:** `Cikan` ≠ `Bamanankan`. Cikan fɔlɔ tɛ se ka Changé; Bamanankan baarakɛcogo ye fɛnw ye minnu bɛ tali kɛ ɲɔgɔn na.
- **Moderasiyɔn:** `Rapport` ≠ `Moderation Desizɔn`. Cikan ye taamasyɛn dɔrɔn de ye; ɲɛmɔgɔ dɔ de bɛ sɛgɛsɛgɛli kɛ.
- **Nafolo:** `Dɛmɛ` ≠ `Nafolo balansi`. Sarali bɛ bila warimarayɔrɔ dɔ la cogo la min tɛ Changé, gafemarayɔrɔ (wari gafe) fɛ.

### Barokun domaniw minnu bɛ ɲɔgɔn kan
Sisεmεti in tilalen don hakilinata-yɔrɔw la (dantigɛlenw): Danyɔrɔ, Jatebɔ, Jɛkulu, Kumaɲɔgɔnya, Sigida, Kan, Moderation, Ladɔnniyali, Nafolo ani Mara. U bɛ cakɛda bɛɛ jira k’a ta ɲɛnajɛla lakikaw la (baarakɛlaw, lakɔlisow, ONGw) ka se u ka nizɛri jɛɲɔgɔnyaw ma.
