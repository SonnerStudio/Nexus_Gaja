I nɔ jɔs de fɔ translet wɔd dɛn wit stɛp, bɔt na fɔ **transfa minin**. Nexus Gaja de kɔnɛkt pipul dɛn pan dip lɛvul bay we i ɔndastand di kɔlchɔ, rijinal ɛn kɔntɛkstual nyuans, we de mek dɛn ebul fɔ tɔk rial, ɔtɛnɛtik.

## Teknik akitekchɔ (kɔr kɔnsɛpt)

![Nɛks Gaja Transleshɔn Kɔnsɛpt](assets/img/nexus_translation.jpg)

Di teknikol kor fɔ Nexus Gaja na wan sɛlf-divɛlɔp kɔmyunikeshɔn mɔdel, we strikt wan sheb to tri layers:

1. **Original**: Di kɔmyunikeshɔn ɔbjɛkt (mɛsej) we di pɔsin we sɛn am mek, nɔ kin chenj ɔltɛm.
2. **Sɛmantik Intɛpriteshɔn**: Di sistɛm nɔ jɔs de analayz di wɔd dɛn, bɔt di aktual minin.
3. **Target langwej ripreshɔn**: Di AI de jɔs mek wan tɛmporari ɔ kesh ripreshɔn fɔ di ɔrijinal fɔ di rispektiv risipiɛnt bays pan dɛn langwej we dɛn lɛk. Transleshɔn dɛn nɔ de ɛva rayt oba di ɔrijinal mɛsej.

### Dipɛnsin fɔ di kɔntekst
Transleshɔn nɔ de ɛva luk nyus na Nexus Gaja insay aysolɛshɔn. Di injin de tek di ɔl hayari na kɔnt:
`Mɛsej` → `Mɛsej dɛn we bin dɔn de bifo` → `Trɛd Kɔntekst` → `Kɔmyuniti Kɔntekst` → `Langwej / Rijyɔn` → `Yuz Prɛferɛns`

### Efisiɛns tru on-dimand transleshɔn
Di transleshɔn de apin nɔmɔ **we dɛn kɔl am** (on-dimand) insay wan we we go sev risɔs. We pɔsin we de yuz am aks fɔ kɔntinyu, dɛn kin translet am to dɛn difɔlt langwej. Dɛn kin kip di transleshɔn fɔ wan patikyula langwej fɔ ɔltɛm (caching) fɔ mek di kwɛstyɔn dɛn we dɛn kin aks leta kwik kwik wan.

## AI-pawad mɔdareshɔn (WP 1.8.4) .

![Nɛks Gaja AI Mɔdareshɔn](asɛt/img/nɛks_mɔdareshɔn.jpg)

Wit AI-sɔpɔt mɔdareshɔn, wi tek wan big stɛp frɔm di prɔdak aydia to di tɛknikal akitɛkɛt ɛn tek kɔnt ɔf di kɔrɛnt EU rigyuleshɔn dɛn (transparency ɔbligayshɔn dɛn fɔ di EU AI Akt akɔdin to At. 50; Dijital Savis Akt wit kɔmprɛhɛnsif jɔstis ɛn opshɔn fɔ ɔbjɛshɔn).

### 1. Besik prinsipul
Di sɛntɛns we impɔtant pas ɔl fɔ di akitɛkɛt na: **Modareshɔn AI na chɛk sistɛm ɛn nɔto ɔtonamɛnt ruling sistɛm.**
I de fɔ sɔpɔt pipul dɛn wit mɔdareshɔn, nɔto fɔ disayd us opinion dɛn alaw fɔ de pan Nexus Gaja.
So wi kin difrɛns bitwin tri tin dɛn:
- **Detect:** “I kin bi se dɛn dɔn pwɛl di lɔ ya.”
- **Rate:** “Fɔ ɛgzampul, di prɔbabiliti fɔ mek pɔsin pwɛl di lɔ na 94%.”
- **Disayd:** “Us akshɔn dɛn go rili tek?”
Di tɔd lɛvul fɔ kɔntrol mɔtalman we siriɔs wan.

### 2. Di modareshɔn AI go bi in yon sabsistim
Insted of wan AI, dem establish wan robust sabsistim:
```tɛks
                 NEXUS GAJA AI MODƐRESHƆN
                          │ we dɛn kɔl
       ┌─────────────────── ┼──────────────────┐
       │ │ │
  Langwej AI Sefty AI Froud AI
       │ │ │
       ├─────────────────── ┴──────────────────┤
       │ │ │
 Transleshɔn Biɛvhɔ Aydentiti
 Analysis Analysis Sayn dɛn we dɛn kin yuz
       │ │ │
       └────────────────────────────────────┘
                      ▼ we dɛn kɔl
               Risk asɛsmɛnt fɔ du sɔntin
                      │ we dɛn kɔl
                      ▼ we dɛn kɔl
               Human Rivyu
```

### 3. Di AI modul dɛn we impɔtant pas ɔl
Nayn spɛshal analisis eria dɛn de fɔ Nexus Gaja:
- **M1 – Langwej Ɔndastandin**: Detekt langwej, dayalɛkt, slang, ayroni indikɛtɔ, transleshɔn prɔblɛm.
- **M2 – Toxicity / Abuse Detection**: Detekt insults, pɔsin in yon atak, harassment.
- **M3 – Threat Detection**: Detekt poshubul threat, blakmɛl, ɛn trɛt fɔ vaylɛns.
- **M4 – Hate / Dehumanization Detection**: Detekt targeted atak pan pipul bays pan spesifik afilieshɔn.
- **M5 – Spam / Manipuleshɔn Ditekshɔn**: Detekt spam, bot bihayvya, kɔdinɛt manipuleshɔn.
- **M6 – Froud Ditekshɔn**: Detekt saspek fɔ frod tray, phishing, soshal injinɛri.
- **M7 – Identity Integrity**: I de chɛk signal dɛn bɔt aw fɔ tek di akɔn, bɔku akɔn, blɔk baypas.
- **M8 – Midia Sefty**: Analayz imej, odio, vidio, dɔkyumɛnt.
- **M9 – Kɔntekst Injin**: Di mɔdyul we impɔtant pas ɔl. I de briŋ di tin dɛn we dɛn dɔn fɛn togɛda.

### 4. Wetin mek di Kɔntekst Injin impɔtant so
Wan klin ki wɔd sɔch nɔ go du fɔ am. “A kin kil am we i de laf” gɛt sɛmantik vaylɛns, bɔt na figa. “Tumara na 8 p.m. a go shot am fo front of im haus” na komplit difren situeshɔn. Di AI fɔ ɔndastand wetin di stetmɛnt min insay dis patikyula kɔntɛks.

### 5. Mɔdareshɔn we de tɔk bɔku langwej dɛn
Mɔdareshɔn nɔ kin jɔs kɔmpia wɔd dɛn. I fɔ analayz di lɛvɛl we minin (e.g. Jaman idiom vs. Japan idiom vs. rijinal ɛksprɛshɔn).

### 6. Ɔrijinal langwej + transleshɔn
Dɛn kin analayz di ɔrijinal ɛn di transleshɔn wan bay wan. Na da tɛm de nɔmɔ di “Kɔmbayn Mɔdareshɔn Asɛsmɛnt” kin apin. Dis we ya, Nexus Gaja kin no if di transleshɔn insɛf go dɔn mek di tin wɔs ɔ chenj.

### 7. Kɔnfidɛns Skɔ
εvri AI asesmεnt de gεt kכnfidεns valyu (e.g. Threat probability: 0.96). Bɔt: **Kɔnfidɛns Skɔ ≠ Tru.** If yu gɛt 96% skɔ, i jɔs min se di mɔdel rili gɛt kɔnfidɛns pan in klasification, nɔto fɔ se di pɔsin we de yuz am gilti.

### 8. Nɔ shɔ insɛf kin bi signal
If di AI nɔ shɔ (e.g. Threat: 0.62, Satire: 0.54), i nɔ kin jɔs tek harsh akshɔn. Bifo dat, dɛn bil ɔnsatayniti dairekt insay di akitɛkɛt: **Human Review Required**.

### 9. Fo disizhɔn eria
- 🟢 **GREEN**: I go mɔs bi se i de fala di lɔ. → nɔ akshɔn.
- 🟡 **YELLOW**: Posisibul violeshɔn. → obshɔb / notis if nid de.
- 🟠 **ORANGE**: Prɔbabl violeshɔn. → Chɛk fɔ di mɔdareshɔn.
- 🔴 **RED**: Big poshubul violeshɔn. → kwik protεktiv mεzhɔ + mכtalman tεst.

### 10. Nɔ “AI penalty” .
**Di AI nɔ de put fayn sankshɔn.** I kin trigɛt imejensi tɛknikal mɛsej (e.g. fɔ kip mɛsej) if siriɔs sikyɔriti kɔnsyans, bɔt di las disizhɔn stil de we dɛn kin chɛk.

### 11. Protektiv mɛsej kin bi ɔtomɛtik
If wan patikyula trɛt de (Threat detected → High confidence → Temporary restriction → Human review → Decision), wi de protɛkt di pɔsin we de trɛtin we wi nɔ mek di AI bi di jɔj.

### 12. Di AI fɔ ebul fɔ jɔstify in disayd
Di DSA nid klia ɛn patikyula rizin dɛn. Di AI rizin dɛn insay wan strɔkchɔ we: Rul (NG-CONDUCT-004), Dɛn dɔn notis (Potensial kɔnkrit trɛt), Kɔnfidɛns (0.94), Rilevɛns kɔntɛks (4 mɛsej dɛn we bin dɔn pas), Rikɔmɛnd akshɔn (Mɔtalman rivyu).

### 13. AI nɔ alaw fɔ chenj kɔntinyu sikrit wan
**Moderation AI nɔ fɔ ɛva chenj di ɔrijinal kɔntinyu we yu nɔ notis.** Ɔtomatik kɔrɛkshɔn, transleshɔn ɔ sɔmarizeshɔn de kip di ɔrijinal kɔntinyu.

### 14. AI jenarayz kɔntinyu
wi difrεnt bitwin: mכtalman kriyayt, AI-asist, AI-jεnarεt εn AI-manipul. Dis kin bi pat pan di kɔntinyu mɛtadata.

### 15. AI Kɔntinɛnt Lɛbul & AI Provenans Lay
Akɔdin to di transparency rul dɛm fɔ di EU AI Act (sins Ɔgɔst 2026), dɛn fɔ mek pipul dɛn no di tin dɛn we AI dɔn mek. Wi de imajin wan AI Provenans Laya we de kip mɛtadata (AI ɔrijin, mɔdel, tɛmstamp, mɔtalman rivyu).

### 16. Dipfake ditekshɔn
Di akitekchɔ na fɔ detekt sintetik imej, stimulated vɔys ɛn dipfakes. Bɔt fɔ no pɔsin nɔto pruf fɔ insɛf.

### 17. Nɔ ɔtomɛtik “tru mashin” (mɔdareshɔn ≠ fɔ chɛk di tru tin dɛn) .
Wan sistem de chɛk: “Di tin dɛn we de insay de pwɛl ɛni lɔ?” (Content Moderation), wan ɔda wan de gi: "Us infɔmeshɔn ɛn sɔs dɛn de?" (Infɔmeshɔn Ɛp). Dɛn nɔ jɔs de dilit di opinion dɛn as “rɔng”.

### 18. Protɛkshɔn agens kɔlchɔ misinterpretation
AI nid **Cultural Context Models** fɔ mek dɛn nɔ adopt wan kɔntri in kɔmyunikeshɔn norm as global standad.

### 19. Ayroni, satire ɛn yumo
Di AI de yuz kɔntɛks, imɔji, tɔk-tɔk flɔ ɛn ayroni strɔkchɔ dɛn we pipul dɛn sabi, bɔt i fɔ alaw fɔ mek dɛn nɔ shɔ we di minin dɛn nɔ klia.

### 20. Nɔ pɔnishmɛnt bays pan wan AI skɔ
Nɔ siriɔs mɔdareshɔn intavɛnshɔn nɔ kin bi bays nɔmɔ pan wan ɔtomatik klasifikeshɔn rizɔlt (Tɛks + Kɔntekst + Biɛvhɔ + Langwej + Midia + Rul Injin = Risk Asɛsmɛnt).

### 21. Yuz Biɛvhɔ Sayn & Nɔ Sɔshial Krɛdit Sistɛm
Na bɔt tɛknikal abiuz signal (e.g. spam mas postin), nɔto jenɛral soshal raytin sistɛm. Nexus Gaja nɔ de ɔpreshɔn soshal kredit sistɛm - mɔdareshɔn na fɔ sikyɔriti ɛn nɔto fɔ asɛs pɔsin in valyu.

### 22. Modareshɔn AI fɔ bi ɔditabl
Ɔl di ɔtomatik disizhɔn dɛn we gɛt fɔ du wit am, dɛn kin log (ivent ID, rul ID, kɔnfidɛns, mɔtalman rivyu, ɛn ɔda tin dɛn) fɔ mek shɔ se dɛn ebul fɔ tray fɔ no am.

### 23. Falz Pozitiv, Falz Negativ & Kwaliti Mɛtrik
Dɛn de wach di kayn mistek dɛn. Wan dashbɔd de mɛzhɔ prɛsishɔn, rikɔl ɛn mɔ di **Apil Rivasal Rɛt** (nɔmba fɔ di apil dɛn we dɔn saksesful). 

### 24. Langwej fayn & transleshɔn bias
Di mɔdareshɔn kwaliti fɔ kɔmparabl akɔdin to ɔl di langwej dɛn we dɛn sɔpɔt (Multilingual Moderation Benchmark). If di modareshɔn rizɔlt difrɛn bitwin di ɔrijinal ɛn di transleshɔn (transleshɔn kɔnflikt), dɛn fɔ chɛk dis wan bay wan.

### 25. Akitekchɔ Prɔpɔzal & Polisi Injin
Di rul dɛm (pɔlisi injin) nɔ gɛt had-kɔd insay di AI mɔdel dɛm. Di AI de gi insayt, di polisi injin de mek disizhɔn bays pan di lɔ dɛn we de naw. Dis de alaw **mɔdel chenj we yu nɔ chenj di lɔ dɛn**.

### 26. Pipul de stil bi di fainal otoriti
- **NG-AI-MOD-001**: Di AI de sɔpɔt ditekshɔn ɛn klasification, bɔt i nɔ de tek ples fɔ mɔtalman rivyu we dɛn de mek siriɔs disizhɔn.
- **NG-AI-MOD-002**: Ɔtomatik mɔdareshɔn disizhɔn dɛn fɔ tray, rɛkɔd ɛn verify.

**Sɔmari**: Wi de bil wan 4-taya sistɛm: AI ditekshɔn, kɔntɛks ɛn risk analisis, polisi injin ɛn mɔtalman gɔvmɛnt. Dis de mek dɛn ebul fɔ mek ɔtomɛshɔn strɔng we nɔ de mek wan denja “AI as jɔj” akitɛkɛt.

## Faynɛns prinsipul ɛn rɛvɛnyu mɔdel (WP 1.10.1) .

![Nɛks Gaja Faynɛns Mɔdel](asɛt/img/nɛks_faynɛns.jpg)

Wan rili impɔtant besik ikɔmik prinsipul de aplay to Nexus Gaja: **No klas advatayzmɛnt insay di pletfɔm.**
Dis mek Nexus Gaja difrɛn frɔm bɔku pan di soshal nɛtwɔk dɛn tide. Bɔt dat nɔ min se: Nexus Gaja nɔ fɔ gɛt kɔmɛshɔnal karakta. Difrɛn frɔm dat: di pletfɔm fɔ bi ikɔnomik wan so dat in soshal pɔpɔshɔn go kɔntinyu fɔ de fɔ lɔng tɛm. So di ikɔnomi aktiviti na we fɔ rich wan ɛnd, nɔto di rial rizin fɔ di pletfɔm.

### 1. Prinsipul NG-FIN-001
Nexus Gaja de faynans in opareshɔn dɛn tru transparent revenyu strim dɛn we separet frɔm di yuza intɛres ɛn nɔto tru di makɛt fɔ in yuza dɛn atɛnshɔn ɔ pɔsin in pasɔnal data.

### 2. No klashik advatayzmɛnt
Speshali, dɛn nɔ go alaw dɛn tin ya:
- Baner advatayzmɛnt
- Pop-ap advatayzmɛnt
- Advatayzmɛnt vidio dɛn we dɛn kin ple ɔtomɛtik wan
- spɔnj post dɛn na di nɔmal fid
- pɔsnalayz advatayzmɛnt prɔfayl dɛn
- Sel yuz profayl
- Sɛl fɔ pɔsin in pasɔnal data
- Advatayzmɛnt we kɔmɔt frɔm prayvet tɔk-tɔk.

Dis de lɛf di kɔmyunikeshɔn spɛs: **Kɔmyunikeshɔn spɛs instead ɔf advatayzmɛnt spɛs.**

### 3. Faynɛns we nɔ gɛt advatayzmɛnt
Bifo dat, di mɔni we wi de gi kin gɛt bɔku pila dɛn. Wi fɔs prɔpos siks pila dɛn:
```tɛks
                 NƐKSƆS GAJA
                     │ we dɛn kɔl
       ┌───────────────────────────┐
       ▼ ▼ ▼ we dɛn kɔl
   PRIMIUM ƆGANAYZƐSHƆN DƆNƐT
       │ │ │
       ├───────────────────────────┤
       ▼ ▼ ▼ we dɛn kɔl
   PROMƆT PATNASHIP SAVIS DƐN
```

#### Pila 1 – Fri besik mɛmbaship
**Nexus Gaja Free:** Besik patisipeshon fo intanashona andastan no shud dipen pan if pesin get moni. Dɛn tin ya kin apin fri: pɔsin in yon prɔfayl, intanashɔnal kɔmyunikeshɔn, kɔntribyushɔn, kɔmɛnt, bɛsik transleshɔn, kɔmyuniti, chat, besik midia wok.

#### Pila 2 – Prɛmiɔm Ɔfa dɛn
Volontia pe fɔ ɔf (**Nexus Gaja Plus**). Di bɛnifit dɛn we pɔsin kin gɛt: big stɔrɔj ​​limit, ay midia kwaliti, lɔng vidio transmishɔn, ɛkspanda transleshɔn wok, big kɔmyuniti, ɔda ɔganayzeshɔnal wok, ay AI kwota.
**Freemium – bɔt nɔto “Dark Freemium”:** Yu kin yuz di fri vɛshɔn wit sɛns; Premium de mek i bɔku.

#### Pila 3 – Ɔganayzeshɔn dɛn
**Nɛks Gaja Ɔganayzeshɔn:** Speshal akɔn fɔ skul, yunivasiti, klab, NGO, kɔmni, munisipaliti, ɛn ɔda tin dɛn, wit sɛntral ɔganayzeshɔnal akɔn, mɛmba manejmɛnt ɛn admin fɛnshɔn dɛn. (Ɛgzampul: Wan skul kin gɛt studɛnt akses fri ɔ tru institiushɔnal tarif).

#### Pila 4 – Doneshɔn dɛn
**Nexus Gaja funding fund:** Di wan dɛn we de gi mɔni kin gi mɔni bay dɛnsɛf (jɛnɔral ɔ iamak doneshɔn). 
**Funding pool ɛn tombola:** Dɛn kin pe fɔ yuz fri ɔ diskɔnt frɔm wan doneshɔn pul. Wan fund alɔkeshɔn lɛdja de mek shɔ se dɛn de mak am.
```tɛks
DƆNƐSHƆN → PƆPƆZ FƆND → ELIGIBILITI ENJIN → Fri akses / Diskɔnt / Kwata
```

#### Pila 5 – Instityushɔnal sɔpɔt
Spɔnsɔ: Fawndeshɔn, kɔlchɔ fɔnd program, stet fɔnd program.
**NG-FIN-002 (Indipɛndɛns):** Fɔnd nɔ kin bay ɛditorial ɔ tɛknikal kɔntrol oba Nexus Gaja.

#### Pila 6 – Kɔmishɔn Savis
Nexus Gaja kin gi B2B savis dɛn lɛk **Translation-as-a-Service (API)**, pɔshɔnal transleshɔn, ɔganayzeshɔnal kɔmyunikeshɔn ɔ intanashɔnal kɔnfrɛns rum dɛn we nɔ go lod di nɔmal yuza fid wit advatayzmɛnt.

### 4. Keine Datenvermarktung und keine „Surveillance Economy“
**NG-FIN-003:** Personenbezogene Nutzerdaten sind kein Handelsgut von Nexus Gaja. (Kein Verkauf von Profilen, Historien, Identitätsdaten). 
Nexus Gaja soll nicht davon profitieren, dass seine Nutzer möglichst viel überwacht und psychologisch möglichst präzise klassifiziert werden.

### 5. Finanzielle Transparenz
Nexus Gaja Financial Transparency: Veröffentlichbar sind Einnahmen nach Kategorien, Spendenvolumen, Betriebskosten etc. 
**Fördermittel-Ledger:** Zweckgebundene Spenden werden buchhalterisch nachvollziehbar erfasst (Donation → Fund ID → Purpose → Available Balance → Allocation). Es gibt keine Quersubventionierung ohne Transparenz.

### 6. Preisgestaltung und Solidarisches Finanzierungsmodell
Die Preise orientieren sich an: Kostenorientierung, Fairness und Solidarität.
**Solidarisches Premium:** Ein Premium-Nutzer kann freiwillig wählen: „Ich finanziere einen Teil des Zugangs eines anderen Nutzers.“ Zwangssolidarität oder eine Premium-Klassengesellschaft (weniger Respekt oder schlechtere Moderation für Free-Nutzer) ist ausgeschlossen.

### 7. Wirtschaftliche KPI

## API, intafeys ɛn kɔmyunikeshɔn akitɛkɛt (WP 1.11.3)

Fɔ mek shɔ se di sistɛm stebul, sikyɔriti ɛn skɛlabiliti, Nexus Gaja de fala wan strikt API-based ɛn ivin-driven akitɛkɛt.

### Besik prinsipul dɛn
- **Nɔ dairekt database akses:** Kɔmpɔnɛnt dɛn de kɔmyuniket jɔs bay difayn intafɛs (API ɔ ivin), nɔ ɛva tru dairekt database kwɛstyɔn frɔm ɔda savis.
- **API Getway:** Ɔl di ɛksternal klaynt riŋwe dɛn de go tru wan API Getway we de handle ɔthɛntishɔn, routin ɛn ret limitin.
- **Prɔvayda abstrakshɔn:** Ɛksternal savis (AI mɔdel, pemɛnt prɔvayda, transleshɔn injin) dɛn intagret via abstrakshɔn layers. Dis de mek di dipɛnsin dɛn we gɛt had-wayd nɔ de ɛn i de alaw fɔ chenj di prɔvayda we kin chenj.

### Kɔmyunikeshɔn patɛns
- **Synchronous APIs (REST/HTTPS):** Dɛn kin yuz am fɔ riŋwe wantɛm wantɛm lɛk fɔ login, profayl sɛtin ɔ dairekt transleshɔn.
- **Asynchronous Events (Event Bus):** Nexus Gaja in sεntri nεv sistεm fכ dikopl prכsεs dεm (e.g. `Message.Created` de trigεr mכdarεshכn, transleshכn εn notis asynchronously).
- **Rial-taym (WebSocket):** Dediket chanɛl fɔ layv chat ɛn tayp stetɔs.

### Sekyuriti ɛn rilaybiliti
- **Zero Trust Model:** Intanɛt nɛtwɔk trafik nɔ de trɔst ɔtomɛtik wan; sɛnsitiv savis-to-savis kɔmyunikeshɔn nid fɔ ɔthɛntishɔn.
- **Idempotence & Outbox Pattern:** Krio opareshɔn (lɛk doneshɔn ɔ mɛsej) dɛn mek fɔ bi idempotent fɔ mek dɛn nɔ du bɔku prɔses ɛn yuz di ɔtbɔks patɛn fɔ mek shɔ se di tin dɛn we apin nɔ lɔs ivin insay database transakshɔn.

## MVP Domɛyn Mɔdel (WP 1.12) .

![Nexus Gaja Modular Monolith](asɛt/img/nɛks_akitekchɔ.jpg)

Nexus Gaja de yuz wan strikt domɛyn-driv MVP akitɛkɛt (ADR-025), we dɛn disayn as modular monolit wit klia domɛyn bɔda. Dis strɔkchɔ de mek di fɔs maykrosavis kɔmplisiti nɔ apin we i de mentɛn di fleksibiliti fɔ ɔtsɔs spɛshal domɛyn dɛn leta.

### Teknik kor ɛntiti dɛn
Di akitɛkɛt de strikt wan separet kɔnsɛpt dɛn fɔ mek shɔ se di data intɛgriti ɛn avɔyd mistek dɛn lɛk “yuz nem = mɔtalman”:
- **Aydentiti & Akɔn:** `Pɔsin` ≠ `Yuz Akɔn` ≠ `Aydɛntiti Vɛrifikɛshɔn`. Pɔsin we dɛn dɔn chɛk kin tek pat tru wan akɔn, bɔt di ɛntiti dɛn kin stil separet.
- **Kɔmyunikeshɔn:** `Mɛsej` ≠ `Transleshɔn`. Di ɔrijinal mɛsej nɔ de chenj; Transleshɔn na tin dɛn we gɛt link.
- **Modareshɔn:** `Ripɔt` ≠ `Modareshɔn Disishɔn`. Mɛsej na jɔs sɔntin we de sho se pɔsin de du sɔntin; wan modarɛta de du di invɛstishɔn.
- **Faynans:** `Dɔneshɔn` ≠ `Fɔnd Balɛns`. Dɛn kin post di pemɛnt dɛn to wan fɔnd we nɔ kin chenj bay we dɛn yuz lɛdja (kɛsh buk).

### Kɔnekt sɔbjɛkt domɛyn dɛn
Di sistɛm sheb to lɔjik domɛyn (baund kɔntɛks): Aydentiti, Akɔn, Ɔganayzeshɔn, Kɔmyunikeshɔn, Kɔmyuniti, Langwej, Mɔdareshɔn, Notis, Faynɛns ɛn Gɔvmɛnt. Dɛn de ripresent di ɔl chen frɔm rial aktɔ dɛn (yuz, skul, NGO) to dɛn dijital intarakshɔn.
