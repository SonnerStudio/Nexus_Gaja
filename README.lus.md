**Nexus Gaja** hi khawvel pum huap inbiakpawhna tidanglam thei tura siam, inbiakpawhna network fing tak, context-aware a ni.

## Tum leh Hmuh theihna

![Nexus Gaja Vision](a thil neih/img/nexus_vision.jpg) tih a ni.

Khawvel pum huap khawvelah hian tawng hi a daltu lian ber a ni fo thin. Nexus Gaja thil tum ber chu mipui inkara inbiakpawhna mumal tak, hmantlak leh context-accurate siam a ni - tawng inang hmang emaw hmang lo emaw pawh nise. 

Thumal rilru na taka lehlin mai ni lovin, ** awmzia inthlak danglam** chungchang a ni. Nexus Gaja hian culture, regional leh contextual nuances hriatthiamna hmangin level thuk zawkah mipui a thlunzawm a, inbiakna tak tak, dik tak a siam thei a ni.

## Thil awm thei leh thil awm dan
- **Multimedia communication**: He system hian text chauh ni lovin, images, audio leh video te pawh a process thei a ni. Hei hian ṭawng ramri kaltlanga inbiakna (e.g. video call emaw voice message emaw) chu a hun takah (fully immersive conversation) a phalsak a ni.
- **Context sensitivity**: Traditional translator-te hriatthiam loh fo thin irony, idioms, jargon leh regional dialect te hmuhchhuah.
- **Cross-platform network**: Private chat, forum thread (comment awmna post) leh khawvel pum huap khawtlang inpawhna atana hmanraw tangkai tak a ni.

## Technical architecture (core concept) chungchang a ni.

![Nexus Gaja lehlinna ngaihdan](assets/img/nexus_translation.jpg)

Nexus Gaja technical core chu mahni inbiakpawhna model a ni a, chu chu khauh takin layer pathum-ah ṭhen a ni a:

1. **Original**: A thawntuin a siam communication object (message) chu a danglam thei lo fo thin.
2. **Semantic Interpretation**: System hian thumal chauh ni lovin, a awmzia tak tak a zirchiang a.
3. **Target language representation**: AI hian an recipient hrang hrangte tan an duhzawng language a zirin original chu temporary emaw cached emaw chauh a siam thin. Lehlin hian original message chu a overwrite ngai lo.

### Context a innghat a ni
Lehlin hian Nexus Gaja-ah hian thuthang chu a hranin an en ngai lo. Engine hian hierarchy zawng zawng a ngaihtuah vek a:
`Message` → `Thuchah hmasa ber` → `Thread Context` → `Community Context` → `Ṭawng / Region` → `User duhzawng`

### On-demand lehlin hmanga efficiency
Lehlin hi **koh hunah** (on-demand) chauhvin resource-saving takin a thleng thin. User pakhatin content a dil chuan an default language-ah a letling a ni. Ṭawng bik atana lehlin chu a hnu lama zawhnate nasa taka ti chak turin a nghetin (caching) dah a ni.

## AI hmanga moderation (WP 1.8.4) a ni.

![Nexus Gaja AI Moderation](assets/img/nexus_moderation.jpg) a ni a, a hlawhtlinna chu a hlawhtlinna a ni.

AI-supported moderation hmang hian product idea a\angin technical architecture-ah hmasawnna lian tak kan nei a, tuna EU dan siamte (Art. 50-a EU AI Act-a transparency obligations; Digital Services Act-a hriatthiam theiha justifications leh options for objection) te kan ngaihtuah a ni.

### 1. Thu bulpui ber
Architecture atana sentence pawimawh ber chu: **Moderation AI hi checking system a ni a, autonomous ruling system a ni lo.**
Mite chu a tlem berah thlawp tumna a ni a, Nexus Gaja chungchangah eng ngaihdan nge awm phal tih hriat tum a ni lo.
Chutiang chuan thil pathum kan thliar hrang a:
- **Detect:** “Hetah hian dan bawhchhiatna a awm thei.”
- **Rate:** “Entirnan, dan bawhchhiatna a awm theihna chu 94% a ni.”
- **Thutlukna siam rawh:** “Eng action nge tih tak tak ang?”
Level pathumna hi thil pawi tak takah chuan mihring lam hawia thunun a ngai a ni.

### 2. Moderation AI chu a subsystem a ni ang
AI pakhat ai chuan subsystem nghet tak din a ni a:
```thuziak
                 NEXUS GAJA AI THIL AWM DAN
                          │ tih a ni
       ┌─────────────────── ┼──────────────────┐
       │ │ │ tih a ni
  Ṭawng AI Hriselna AI Bumna AI
       │ │ │ tih a ni
       ├─────────────────── ┴──────────────────┤
       │ │ │ tih a ni
 Lehlin nungchang Identity
 Analysis thlirletna Signal te
       │ │ │ tih a ni
       └────────────────────────────────────
                      ▼ tih a ni
               Risk assessment neih a ni
                      │ tih a ni
                      ▼ tih a ni
               Mihringte thlirletna
```

### 3. AI module pawimawh ber berte
Nexus Gaja tan hian specialized analysis area 9 a awm a, chungte chu:
- **M1 – Ṭawng Hriatthiamna**: Ṭawng, tawng, slang, irony indicator, lehlin lama harsatna awmte a hmuchhuak.
- **M2 – Toxicity / Abuse Detection**: Insult, mimal beihna, tihduhdahna te a hmuchhuak.
- **M3 – Threat Detection**: Threat, blackmail, leh tharum thawhna thlen thei te a hmuchhuak.
- **M4 – Hate / Dehumanization Detection**: Mite chunga targeted attack te chu affiliation bik a zirin a detect thin.
- **M5 – Spam / Manipulation Detection**: Spam, bot awm dan, coordinated manipulation te a hmuchhuak.
- **M6 – Fraud Detection**: Bum tumna rinhlelh awm, phishing, social engineering te a hmuchhuak.
- **M7 – Identity Integrity**: Account lak dan, account tam tak, block bypass chungchanga signal awmte a enfiah thin.
- **M8 – Media Safety**: Thlalak, audio, video, document te a zirchiang thin.
- **M9 – Context Engine**: Module pawimawh ber a ni. Mimal thil hmuhchhuahte chu a rawn keng khawm a ni.

### 4. Context Engine hi a pawimawh em em chhan
Keyword search thianghlim tak mai chu a tawk lo ang. “Nui chungin ka that thei” tih hian semantic violence a keng tel a, mahse figure of speech a ni. “Naktuk dar 8 p.m. hian a in hmaah ka kap ang” tih hi thil awmdan danglam tak a ni. AI hian he thumal bik hian eng nge a sawi tum tih a hrethiam tur a ni.

### 5. Ṭawng hrang hrang hmanga moderation
Moderation hian thumal a tehkhin mai thei lo. A awmzia level (e.g. German idioms vs. Japanese idioms vs. regional expressions) a zirchiang tur a ni.

### 6. Original tawng + lehlin
A original leh lehlin chu a hranin kan zirchiang a. Chumi hnuah chauh chuan “Combined Moderation Assessment” chu a thleng ta a ni. Chutiang chuan Nexus Gaja chuan lehlinna ngei pawh hian thil awmdan a tizual emaw, a tidanglam emaw a ni thei tih a hre thei a ni.

### 7. Inrintawkna Score
AI assessment tin hian rintlakna hlutna (e.g. Threat probability: 0.96) a dawng thin. Mahse: **Confidence Score ≠ Thudik.** Score 96% a nih chuan model hian a classification-ah a inrintawk hle tihna chauh a ni a, a hmangtu chu a sual tihna a ni hauh lo.

### 8. Thil chiang lo tak tak chu signal a lo ni ta a ni
AI hi a chiang lo (e.g. Threat: 0.62, Satire: 0.54) a nih chuan, na takin hma a la mai thei lo. Chu ai chuan rinhlelhna chu architecture-ah direct-in a insiam a ni: **Human Review Required**.

### 9. Thutlukna siamna hmun pali
- 🟢 **GREEN**: A zawm ber a rinawm. → thiltih loh.
- 🟡 **YELLOW**: Dan bawhchhiatna a awm thei. → a tul chuan enfiah / chhinchhiah rawh.
- 🟠 **ORANGE**: Dan bawhchhiatna a awm theih. → Moderation check a ni.
- 🔴 **RED**: Dan bawhchhiatna lian tak a awm thei. → venhimna nghal + mihring test.

### 10. “AI penalty” a awm lo.
**AI hian hremna hnuhnung ber a siam lo.** Security lama harsatna lian tak a awm chuan emergency technical measures (e.g. withhold message) a tichhuak thei a, mahse thutlukna hnuhnung ber erawh chu finfiah theih a la ni.

### 11. Invenna hi automatic a ni thei
Threat bik a awm chuan (Threat detected → High confidence → Temporary restriction → Human review → Decision), AI chu judge-ah siam lovin threatened user chu kan humhim a ni.

### 12. AI hian a thutlukna chu a dikna a siam thei tur a ni
DSA hian chhan chiang leh chiang tak a mamawh a ni. AI hian a chhan chu a structured way-in a siam a: Rule (NG-CONDUCT-004), Detected (Potential concrete threat), Confidence (0.94), Relevant context (Thuchah hmasa 4), Recommended action (Mihring thlirletna).

### 13. AI hian a rukin content thlak phal a ni lo
**Moderation AI hian hriat lohvin a original content chu a thlak ngai lo tur a ni.** Automatic correction, translation emaw summarization emaw chuan original content chu a vawng reng thin.

### 14. AI hmanga siam chhuah content
Kan thliar hrang a: mihring siam, AI puihna, AI siam leh AI hmanga siam. Hei hi content metadata-ah a tel ve ta a ni.

### 15. AI Content Labeling & AI lo chhuahna Layer
EU AI Act-a transparency rules (August 2026 aṭang khan) ang chuan AI-in a siam chhuah content chu hriat hlawh tura siam a ngai a ni. Metadata (AI origin, model, timestamp, human review) dahkhawmna tur AI Provenance Layer kan ngaihtuah a ni.

### 16. Deepfake hmuhchhuah
Architecture hi synthetic images, stimulated voices leh deepfakes te hriatchhuahna tur a ni. Mahse hriatna hi automatic proof a ni lo.

### 17. “Truth machine” automatic a awm lo (moderation ≠ fact checking) .
System pakhat chuan heti hian a check a: “Content hian dan eng emaw a bawhchhia em?” (Content Moderation), pakhat chuan heti hian a pe a: "Eng thu leh hla leh source nge awm?" (Information tanpuina) a ni. Ngaihdante chu “dik lo” tia delete mai mai a ni lo.

### 18. Cultural hrilhfiahna dik lo laka invenna
AI hian ram pakhat inbiakpawhna dan chu khawvel pum huap standard anga hman a nih loh nan **Cultural Context Models** a mamawh a ni.

### 19. Irony, satire leh humour
AI hian context, emojis, conversation flows leh irony structure hriat hlawh tak tak a hmang a, mahse awmzia chiang lo a nih chuan rinhlelhna a phal tur a ni.

### 20. AI score pakhat atanga hremna pek loh
Moderation intervention serious tak reng reng hi automated classification result pakhat chauh (Text + Context + Behavior + Language + Media + Rule Engine = Risk Assessment) atanga chhut theih a ni lo.

### 21. User Behavior Signals & Social Credit System awm lo
Technical abuse signal (e.g. spam mass posting) chungchang a ni a, social rating system tlangpui a ni lo. Nexus Gaja hian social credit system a kalpui lo - moderation hi security atan a ni a, mi hlutna tehna atan a ni lo.

### 22. Moderation AI hi audit theih a ni tur a ni
Automated decision kaihhnawih zawng zawng chu logged vek a ni (event ID, rule ID, confidence, human review, etc.) chu traceability a nih theih nan.

### 23. Positive dik lo, Negative dik lo & Quality Metrics te
Error chi hrang hrang enfiah a ni. Dashboard hian precision, recall leh a bik takin **Appeal Reversal Rate** (appeal hlawhtling zat) a teh thin. 

### 24. Ṭawng dikna & lehlin lama bias
Moderation quality hi supported language zawng zawngah tehkhin theih a ni tur a ni (Multilingual Moderation Benchmark). Moderation result original leh lehlin inkara a danglam chuan (lehlin inhnialna) hei hi a hranin enfiah a ngai a ni.

### 25. Architecture ruahmanna leh Policy Engine
Rules (policy engine) hi AI model-ah hian hard-code a ni lo. AI hian insights a pe a, policy engine hian tuna dan awm mek hmangin thutlukna a siam thin. Hei hian dan thlak danglam lovin **model thlak danglam** a phalsak a ni.

### 26. Mite chu thuneihna hnuhnung ber an ni reng
- **NG-AI-MOD-001**: AI hian detection leh classification a thlawp a, mahse thutlukna pawimawh tak siam hunah mihring review a thlak lo.
- **NG-AI-MOD-002**: Automated moderation chungchanga thutlukna siamte chu zawn chhuah theih, record theih leh finfiah theih a ni tur a ni.

**Thu tawi**: Tier pali nei system kan siam mek a, chungte chu AI detection, context leh risk analysis, policy engine leh human governance te an ni. Hei hian “AI as judge” architecture hlauhawm tak siam lovin automation chak tak a siam thei a ni.

## Financing principle leh sum lakluh dan tur model (WP 1.10.1) .

![Nexus Gaja Finance Model](a thil neih/img/nexus_finance.jpg) a ni a, a man pawh a to hle.

Hei hian Nexus Gaja hi tunlai social network tam tak aiin conceptual-ah a danglam hle. Mahse chu chu a awmzia a ni lo: Nexus Gaja hian sumdawnna lama mizia a nei lo tur a ni. Chutih rualin: platform chu a social purpose chu hun rei tak chhunga a awm chhunzawm theih nan economically viable a ni tur a ni. Chuvangin economic activity hi thil tum pakhat thlenna hmanrua a ni a, platform thil tum tak tak a ni lo.

### 1. Thu bul NG-FIN-001
Nexus Gaja hian a hnathawhna tur sum hi a hmangtute ngaihvenna emaw, mimal data emaw hralhna hmang lovin, user interest aṭanga inṭhen, transparent revenue stream hmangin a finance a ni.

## API, interface leh inbiakpawhna architecture (WP 1.11.3)

System stability, security leh scalability neih theihna turin Nexus Gaja hian API-based leh event-driven architecture khauh tak a zawm a ni.

### Thu bulpui ber berte
- **Direct database access nei lo:** Component te hian defined interface (API emaw events emaw hmang chauhin an inbiak a, service dang atanga direct database query hmangin an inbiak ngai lo.
- **API Gateway:** External client request zawng zawng hi API Gateway kaltlangin a kal a, chu chuan authentication, routing leh rate limiting a buaipui thin.
- **Provider abstraction:** Pawn lam service (AI model, payment provider, lehlin engine) te chu abstraction layer hmanga inzawmkhawm a ni. Hei hian hard-wired dependency a veng a, provider switching pawh a ti awlsam bawk.

### Inbiakpawhna kalphung
- **Synchronous APIs (REST/HTTPS):** Login, profile settings emaw direct translation ang chi request nghal tur atan hman a ni.
- **Asynchronous Events (Event Bus):** Nexus Gaja-a central nervous system chu decoupled process (e.g. `Message.Created` hian moderation, translation leh notification asynchronous-in a tichhuak) a ni.
- **Real-time (WebSocket):** Live chat leh type status atana channel bik.

### Hriselna leh rintlakna
- **Zero Trust Model:** Internal network traffic hi a rintlak loh; service-to-service inbiakpawhna sensitive takah chuan authentication a ngai a ni.
- **Idempotence & Outbox Pattern:** Critical operations (donation emaw message ang chi) hi multiple processing venna atana idempotent anga siam a ni a, outbox pattern hmangin database transaction-ah pawh events a bo loh nan hman a ni.

## MVP Domain Model (WP 1.12) hmanga siam a ni.

![Nexus Gaja Modular Monolith](a thil neih/img/nexus_architecture.jpg) a ni a, a man pawh a tlawm hle.

Nexus Gaja hian strictly domain-driven MVP architecture (ADR-025) a hmang a, modular monolith anga design niin domain boundary chiang tak a nei a ni. He structure hian microservice complexity hmasa ber a veng a, chutih rualin a hnua domain bik outsource theihna flexibility a vawng reng bawk.

### Technical lama thil pawimawh ber berte
Architecture hian data integrity a awm theih nan leh “username = human” tih ang chi tihsual a awm loh nan concept hrang hrangte chu khauh takin a thliar hrang a:
- **Identity & Accounts:** `Mihring` ≠ `User Account` ≠ `Identity finfiahna`. Verified person chuan account pakhat hmangin a tel a, mahse entity te chu a hranin an awm thung.
- **Inbiakpawhna:** `Thuchah` ≠ `Lehlin`. Thuchah bulpui chu a danglam thei lo va; Lehlin hi linked entity a ni.
- **Moderation:** `Report` ≠ `Moderation chungchanga thutlukna`. Thuchah chu a hriattirna mai a ni a; moderator pakhatin chhui chianna a kalpui thin.
- **Sum leh pai:** `Thilpek` ≠ `Fund Balance`. Pawisa pekte chu thlak danglam ngai lovin ledger (cash book) hmangin fund-ah post a ni.

### Subject domain inzawmkhawm
System hi logical domain (bounded contexts) ah then a ni a, chungte chu Identity, Account, Organization, Communication, Community, Language, Moderation, Notification, Finance leh Governance te an ni. Anni hian actors tak tak (users, schools, NGOs) atanga an digital interaction thlengin chain pumpui an entir a ni.
