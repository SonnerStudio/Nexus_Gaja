## Cholinga ndi Masomphenya

![Nexus Gaja Vision](assets/img/nexus_vision.jpg)

M’dziko logwirizana kwambiri, chinenero ndicho chopinga chachikulu kwambiri. Cholinga chachikulu cha Nexus Gaja ndikupangitsa kuti anthu azilankhulana mopanda msoko, opezeka komanso olondola pamitu - mosasamala kanthu kuti amalankhula chilankhulo chofala kapena ayi. 

Sikuti kungomasulira mawu mouma khosi, koma ndi **kutumiza tanthauzo**. Nexus Gaja imalumikiza anthu mozama pomvetsetsa zikhalidwe, madera ndi zochitika, kuthandizira zokambirana zenizeni, zenizeni.

## Zotheka ndi mawonekedwe
- **Kulankhulana kwamitundu yambiri **: Dongosolo limayendetsa osati zolemba zokha, komanso zithunzi, zomvera ndi makanema. Izi zimalola kukambirana mozama (monga kuyimbira pavidiyo kapena kutumizirana mauthenga) munthawi yeniyeni kudutsa malire a zilankhulo.
- **Kukhudzika kwa nkhani**: Kuzindikira zachipongwe, miyambi, mawu am'kamwa ndi zilankhulo zachigawo zomwe nthawi zambiri sizimamveka bwino ndi omasulira achikhalidwe.
- **Netiweki ya nsanja**: Imakhala ngati maziko ochezera achinsinsi, ulusi wapabwalo (zolemba zokhala ndi ndemanga) komanso kulumikizana kwapadziko lonse lapansi.

## Zomangamanga zaukadaulo (lingaliro loyambira)

![Lingaliro Lomasulira la Nexus Gaja](assets/img/nexus_translation.jpg)

Pakatikati mwaukadaulo wa Nexus Gaja ndi njira yolumikizirana yodzipangira yokha, yomwe imagawidwa m'magawo atatu:

1. **Choyambirira**: Chinthu choyankhulirana (uthenga) chopangidwa ndi wotumiza nthawi zonse chimakhala chosasinthika.
2. **Kutanthauzira Kwamawu**: Dongosolo limasanthula osati mawu okha, koma tanthauzo lenileni.
3. **Chiyimiliro cha chilankhulo chomwe akulowera**: The AI ​​​​imangopanga chifaniziro chakanthawi kapena chosungidwa chaomwe akuwalandira kutengera chilankhulo chomwe amakonda. Zomasulira sizimachotsa uthenga woyambirira.

### Kudalira kwazinthu
Zomasulira sizimayang'ana nkhani mu Nexus Gaja payokha. Injini imaganizira za utsogoleri wonse:
`Uthenga` → `Mauthenga Akale` → `Mawu Amithenga` → `Makhalidwe Adera` → `Chinenero / Chigawo` → `Zokonda Zogwiritsa Ntchito`

### Kuchita bwino pakumasulira komwe mukufuna
Kumasulira kumangochitika **pamene kutchedwa** (pofuna) m'njira yopulumutsa. Wogwiritsa ntchito akapempha zomwe zili, zimamasuliridwa m'chilankhulo chawo chosasinthika. Zomasulira za chilankhulo china zimasungidwa kwamuyaya (caching) kuti mafunso amtsogolo afulumizitse.

### 4. Chifukwa chiyani Context Engine ndi yofunika kwambiri
Kusaka kwa mawu osakira sikungakhale kokwanira. "Ndikhoza kumupha iye akuseka" liri ndi chiwawa cha semantic, koma ndi mawu ophiphiritsa. “Mawa pa 8 p.m. ndidzamuwombera kutsogolo kwa nyumba yake” ndi mkhalidwe wosiyana kotheratu. AI iyenera kumvetsetsa zomwe mawuwo akutanthauza pankhaniyi.

### 5. Kuwongolera zinenero zambiri
Kudziletsa sikungangoyerekeza mawu. Iyenera kusanthula mulingo wa matanthauzo (monga miyambi yachijeremani motsutsana ndi miyambi yachijapani motsutsana ndi mawu achigawo).

### 6. Chiyankhulo choyambirira + kumasulira
Zoyambirira ndi zomasulira zimawunikidwa mosiyana. Pokhapokha m'pamene "Combined Moderation Assessment" ikuchitika. Mwanjira iyi, Nexus Gaja imatha kudziwa ngati kumasulira komweko kungakhale kokulirapo kapena kusintha zinthu.

### 7. Chikhulupiriro chambiri
Kuwunika kulikonse kwa AI kumalandira mtengo wodalirika (mwachitsanzo, Kuwopsa: 0.96). Koma: **Chikhulupiriro Score ≠ Choonadi.** Kuchuluka kwa 96% kumangotanthauza kuti chitsanzocho chili ndi chidaliro kwambiri pamagulu ake, osati kuti wogwiritsa ntchitoyo ndi wolakwa.

### 8. Kusatsimikizika pakokha kumakhala chizindikiro
Ngati AI ndi yosatsimikizika (mwachitsanzo Kuopseza: 0.62, Satire: 0.54), sikungangochitapo kanthu mwankhanza. M'malo mwake, kusatsimikizika kumamangidwa mwachindunji muzomangamanga: **Kuwunika Kwaumunthu Kofunikira **.

### 9. Magawo anayi azisankho
- 🟢 **GREEN**: Zoyenera kutsatira. → palibe chochita.
- 🟡 **YELOW**: Kuphwanya komwe kungachitike. → onani / zindikirani ngati kuli kofunikira.
- 🟠 **ORANGE**: Mwina kuphwanya. → cheke chowongolera.
- 🔴 **KUFIIRA**: Kuphwanya kwakukulu komwe kungatheke. → muyeso wachangu wachitetezo + kuyesa kwa anthu.

### 10. Palibe "AI chilango"
**AI siyimaika zilango zomaliza.** Ikhoza kuyambitsa njira zaukadaulo zadzidzidzi (monga kusagwira uthenga) pakakhala vuto lalikulu lachitetezo, koma chigamulo chomaliza chikhala chotsimikizika.

### 11. Njira zodzitetezera zitha kukhala zokha
Ngati pali chiwopsezo chapadera (Chiwopsezo chapezeka → Kudalira kwakukulu → Kuletsa kwakanthawi → Ndemanga ya anthu → Chigamulo), timateteza wogwiritsa ntchito yemwe akuwopsezedwa popanda kupanga AI kukhala woweruza.

### 12. AI iyenera kutsimikizira chisankho chake
DSA imafuna zifukwa zomveka komanso zenizeni. Zifukwa za AI m'njira yokhazikika: Rule (NG-CONDUCT-004), Kuzindikiridwa (Kuwopseza konkriti), Chidaliro (0.94), Zoyenerana (Mauthenga 4 apitawa), Zochita zolimbikitsidwa (Kuwunika kwaumunthu).

### 13. AI saloledwa kusintha zinthu mwachinsinsi
**Moderation AI siyenera kusintha zomwe zidakhazikitsidwa mosazindikirika.** Zokonza zokha, zomasulira kapena chidule cha mawu amasunga zomwe zili zoyambirira.

### 14. AI adapanga zinthu
Timasiyanitsa pakati pa: zopangidwa ndi anthu, zothandizidwa ndi AI, zopangidwa ndi AI ndi zoyendetsedwa ndi AI. Izi zimakhala gawo la metadata.

### 15. AI Content Labeling & AI Provenance Layer
Malinga ndi malamulo a EU AI Act (kuyambira Ogasiti 2026), zomwe zimapangidwa ndi AI ziyenera kuzindikirika. Tikuganiza za AI Provenance Layer yomwe imasunga metadata (chiyambi cha AI, chitsanzo, sitampu yanthawi, kuwunika kwamunthu).

### 16. Kuzindikira kozama
Zomangamangazo zimapangidwira kuti zizindikire zithunzi zopangidwa, mawu olimbikitsa komanso zozama. Koma kuzindikira si umboni wokha.

### 17. Palibe “makina owona” (moderation ≠ kuwona zenizeni)
Dongosolo limafufuza: "Kodi zomwe zili zikuphwanya malamulo aliwonse?" (Content Moderation), ina ikupereka: "Ndi chidziwitso ndi magwero otani?" (Thandizo la Information). Malingaliro samangochotsedwa ngati "olakwika".

### 18. Chitetezo ku kutanthauzira kolakwika kwa chikhalidwe
AI imafuna **Cultural Context Models** kuti aletse kulumikizana kwa dziko limodzi kuti zisagwiritsidwe ntchito ngati mulingo wapadziko lonse lapansi.

### 19. Zoseketsa, zachipongwe komanso nthabwala
AI imagwiritsa ntchito nkhani, ma emojis, kutuluka kwa zokambirana ndi machitidwe odziwika bwino, koma ayenera kulola kusatsimikizika pamene matanthauzo samveka bwino.

### 20. Palibe chilango chotengera chigoli chimodzi cha AI
Palibe kulowererapo kwakukulu komwe kungakhazikitsidwe pachotsatira chimodzi chokha (Text + Context + Behavior + Language + Media + Rule Engine = Risk Assessment).

### 21. Zizindikiro Zogwiritsira Ntchito & Palibe Social Credit System
Ndi za ma sigino a ukadaulo (monga kutumiza ma sipamu ambiri), osati kachitidwe wamba. Nexus Gaja sagwiritsa ntchito njira yobwereketsa ngongole - kuwongolera ndichitetezo osati kuwunika kufunikira kwa munthu.

### 22. Moderation AI iyenera kuwerengedwa
Zosankha zonse zofunikira zokha zimayikidwa (ID ya chochitika, ID yalamulo, chidaliro, kuwunika kwamunthu, ndi zina zotero) kuti zitsimikizire kuti zikuyenda bwino.

### 24. Chilungamo m'zinenero & kukondera kumasulira
Ubwino wa kusamalitsa uyenera kufananizidwa ndi zilankhulo zonse zothandizidwa (Mullingual Moderation Benchmark). Ngati zotsatira zakusamalitsa zikusiyana pakati pa zoyamba ndi zomasulira (kusemphana ndi matanthauzidwe), izi ziyenera kufufuzidwa mosiyana.

### 25. Zolinga Zomangamanga & Engine Policy
Malamulo (injini yandondomeko) sali okhazikika mumitundu ya AI. AI imapereka zidziwitso, injini yamalamulo imapanga zisankho kutengera malamulo apano. Izi zimathandiza ** kusintha kwachitsanzo popanda kusintha malamulo **.

### 26. Anthu amakhalabe ndi ulamuliro womaliza
- **NG-AI-MOD-001**: AI imathandizira kuzindikira ndi kugawa, koma sikulowa m'malo mwa ndemanga ya anthu popanga zisankho zazikulu.
**NG-AI-MOD-002**: Zosankha zodziyimira pawokha ziyenera kutsatiridwa, zojambulidwa komanso zotsimikizika.

**Chidule **: Tikumanga dongosolo la magawo anayi: Kuzindikira kwa AI, kusanthula zochitika ndi zoopsa, injini yamalamulo ndi utsogoleri wa anthu. Izi zimathandizira ma automation amphamvu popanda kupanga zomanga zowopsa za "AI monga woweruza".

## API, mawonekedwe ndi kulumikizana (WP 1.11.3)

Kuwonetsetsa kuti dongosolo lakhazikika, chitetezo ndi scalability, Nexus Gaja imatsatira mosamalitsa kamangidwe ka API komanso koyendetsedwa ndi zochitika.

### Mfundo zoyambirira
- **Palibe mwayi wofikira pankhokwe wachindunji:** Magawo amalumikizana mwachindunji kudzera m'malo ofotokozera (ma API kapena zochitika), osati kudzera pamafunso achindunji kuchokera kuzinthu zina.
- ** API Gateway:** Zopempha zonse zamakasitomala akunja zimadutsa pa API Gateway yomwe imayang'anira kutsimikizika, mayendedwe ndi kuchepetsa mitengo.
- **Chidule cha opereka:** Ntchito zakunja (mitundu ya AI, opereka malipiro, mainjini omasulira) amaphatikizidwa kudzera mu zigawo zongowonjezera. Izi zimalepheretsa kudalira kwa mawaya olimba ndikulola kusintha kosinthika kwa othandizira.

### Njira zolumikizirana
- **Synchronous APIs (REST/HTTPS):** Amagwiritsidwa ntchito pofunsira nthawi yomweyo monga kulowa, zoikamo mbiri kapena kumasulira mwachindunji.
- **Zochitika Zosasinthika (Mabasi Ochitika):** Nexus Gaja's central nervous system for decoupled procedures (monga `Message.Created` imayambitsa kuwongolera, kumasulira ndi chidziwitso mosagwirizana).
- **Real-time (WebSocket):** Njira zodzipatulira zamacheza amoyo ndi kulemba.

### Chitetezo ndi kudalirika
- ** Zero Trust Model: ** Magalimoto apakati pamaneti sadali odalirika; kuyankhulana kwachinsinsi kwa utumiki ndi ntchito kumafuna kutsimikiziridwa.
- **Kusazindikira & Mawonekedwe a Mabokosi Otuluka:** Zochita zovuta (monga zopereka kapena mauthenga) zidapangidwa kuti zikhale zopanda mphamvu kuletsa kusinthidwa kangapo ndikugwiritsa ntchito mawonekedwe a bokosi lotuluka kuwonetsetsa kuti zochitika sizikutayika ngakhale muzochita za database.

- **Identity & Accounts:** `Munthu` ≠ `Akaunti Yogwiritsa` ≠ `Kutsimikizira Identity`. Munthu wotsimikizika amatenga nawo gawo kudzera muakaunti imodzi, koma mabungwe amakhala osiyana.
- **Kulankhulana:** `Uthenga` ≠ `Kumasulira`. Uthenga wapachiyambi ndi wosasinthika; Zomasulira ndi mabungwe olumikizidwa.
- **Moderation:** `Ripoti` ≠ `Sankhani Moderation`. Uthenga ndi chisonyezo chabe; woyang'anira amafufuza.
- **Ndalama:** `Zopereka` ≠ `Ndalama zandalama`. Malipiro amatumizidwa mosasinthika ku thumba kudzera pa ledger (cash book).
