## Intego n'Icyerekezo

! [Icyerekezo cya Nexus Gaja] (umutungo / img / nexus_vision.jpg)

Mwisi yisi yose, ururimi akenshi ni inzitizi nini. Intego nyamukuru ya Nexus Gaja nugushoboza itumanaho ridasubirwaho, ryoroshye kandi rihuza imvugo hagati yabantu - tutitaye ko bavuga ururimi rumwe cyangwa batavuga. 

Ntabwo ari uguhindura amagambo yinangiye gusa, ahubwo ni ** kwimura ibisobanuro **. Nexus Gaja ihuza abantu kurwego rwimbitse basobanukirwa nu muco, uturere ndetse n’imiterere, bigafasha ibiganiro nyabyo, byukuri.

## Ibishoboka nibiranga
- ** Itumanaho rya Multimedia **: Sisitemu ntabwo ikora inyandiko gusa, ahubwo ikora amashusho, amajwi na videwo. Ibi bituma ibiganiro byuzuye (urugero: guhamagara videwo cyangwa ubutumwa bwijwi) mugihe nyacyo kurenga imbibi zururimi.
- ** Context sensitivite **: Kumenya ibyuma, imvugo, jargon n'imvugo yo mukarere bikunze kutumvikana nabasemuzi gakondo.
- ** Umuyoboro uhuza **

## Ubwubatsi bwa tekiniki (igitekerezo cyibanze)

! [Igitekerezo cyo Guhindura Nexus Gaja] (umutungo / img / nexus_translation.jpg)

Tekiniki ya tekinike ya Nexus Gaja nicyitegererezo cyitumanaho cyitezimbere, kigabanijwemo ibice bitatu:

1. ** Umwimerere **: Ikintu cyitumanaho (ubutumwa) cyakozwe nuwohereje burigihe ntigihinduka.
2. ** Ibisobanuro bya Semantic **: Sisitemu ntabwo isesengura amagambo gusa, ahubwo ibisobanuro nyabyo.
3 .. Ubuhinduzi ntabwo bwandika ubutumwa bwumwimerere.

### Imiterere ishingiye
Ubuhinduzi ntibureba amakuru muri Nexus Gaja mu bwigunge. Moteri ifata urwego rwose:
`Ubutumwa` →` Ubutumwa bwambere` → `Imiterere yinsanganyamatsiko` →` Imiterere yabaturage` → `Ururimi / Akarere` →` Ibyifuzo byabakoresha`

### Gukora neza binyuze mubisobanuro bisabwa
Ubusobanuro bubaho gusa ** iyo bwiswe ** (kubisabwa) muburyo bwo kuzigama umutungo. Iyo umukoresha asabye ibirimo, bihindurwa mururimi rwabo rusanzwe. Ubuhinduzi bwururimi runaka bubitswe burundu (cishing) kugirango byihute kuburyo bwihuse ibibazo nyuma.

## Kugereranya imbaraga za AI (WP 1.8.4)

! [Nexus Gaja AI Moderation] (umutungo / img / nexus_moderation.jpg)

Hamwe na AI ishyigikiwe na AI, dutera intambwe nini kuva mubitekerezo byibicuruzwa kugeza mubuhanga bwa tekiniki kandi tuzirikana amabwiriza y’ubumwe bw’ibihugu by’Uburayi (inshingano zo gukorera mu mucyo mu itegeko ry’ibihugu by’Uburayi hakurikijwe ingingo ya 50;

### 1. Ihame shingiro
Interuro yingenzi kubwubatsi ni: ** Kugereranya AI ni sisitemu yo kugenzura ntabwo ari gahunda yigenga. **
Igamije gushyigikira abantu mu rugero, ntabwo ari ukumenya ibitekerezo byemewe kubaho kuri Nexus Gaja.
Dutandukanya rero ibintu bitatu:
- ** Menya: ** “Hano hashobora kubaho kurenga ku mategeko.”
- ** Igipimo: ** “Urugero, amahirwe yo kurenga ku mategeko ni 94%.”
- ** Fata icyemezo: ** “Ni ikihe gikorwa kizakorwa koko?”
Urwego rwa gatatu rugomba kugenzurwa nubumuntu mubihe bikomeye.

### 2. Kugereranya AI bizaba sisitemu yayo
Mu mwanya wa AI imwe, hashyizweho sisitemu ikomeye:
`` inyandiko
                 NEXUS GAJA MODERATION
                          │
       ┌────────────────── ┼──────────────────┐
       │ │ │
  Ururimi AI Umutekano AI Uburiganya AI
       │ │ │
       ├──────────────┬─── ┴──────────────┬───┤
       │ │ │
 Indangamuntu Imyitwarire
 Isesengura ryibimenyetso
       │ │ │
       └──────────────┼──────────────────┘
                      ▼
               Gusuzuma ingaruka
                      │
                      ▼
               Isubiramo ryabantu
`` `

### 3. Module yingenzi ya AI
Hariho icyenda cyihariye cyo gusesengura Nexus Gaja:
- ** M1 - Gusobanukirwa Ururimi **: Itahura ururimi, imvugo, imvugo, ibipimo byerekana, ibibazo byubuhinduzi.
- ** M2 - Uburozi / Gutahura nabi **: Kumenya ibitutsi, ibitero byumuntu, gutotezwa.
- ** M3 - Kumenya iterabwoba **: Itahura iterabwoba rishoboka, gusebanya, n’iterabwoba.
- ** M4 - Kwanga / Gutakaza Ubumuntu **: Kumenya ibitero byibasiye abantu ukurikije aho bihurira.
- ** M5 - Kugaragaza Spam / Gukoresha **: Kumenya spam, imyitwarire ya bot, guhuza ibikorwa.
- ** M6 - Kumenya uburiganya **: Itahura kugerageza gukekwaho uburiganya, uburobyi, ubwubatsi.
- ** M7 - Ubunyangamugayo **: Kugenzura ibimenyetso bijyanye no gufata konti, konti nyinshi, guhagarika bypass.
- ** M8 - Umutekano w'itangazamakuru **: Gusesengura amashusho, amajwi, amashusho, inyandiko.
- ** M9 - Moteri ya Context **: Module yingenzi. Ihuza ibyabonetse kugiti cye.

### 4. Impamvu moteri ya Context ari ngombwa
Ijambo ryibanze ryishakisha ryaba ridahagije. "Nshobora kumwica aseka" ikubiyemo urugomo rusobanutse, ariko ni imvugo. "Ejo saa munani nzamurasa imbere y'urugo rwe" ni ibintu bitandukanye rwose. AI igomba kumva icyo ayo magambo asobanura muriki gice cyihariye.

### 5. Kugereranya indimi nyinshi
Gushyira mu gaciro ntibishobora kugereranya amagambo gusa. Igomba gusesengura urwego rwibisobanuro (urugero: imvugo yubudage nu mvugo yikiyapani n imvugo yakarere).

### 6. Ururimi rwumwimerere + ibisobanuro
Umwimerere nubusobanuro byasesenguwe bitandukanye. Icyo gihe ni bwo habaho "Isuzumabumenyi Ryagereranijwe". Muri ubu buryo, Nexus Gaja irashobora kumenya niba ubusobanuro ubwabwo bushobora kuba bwarushijeho kwiyongera cyangwa guhindura ibintu.

### 7. Amanota yo Kwizera
Buri suzuma rya AI ryakira agaciro kizere (urugero: Iterabwoba rishoboka: 0.96). Ariko: ** Amanota Yizere ≠ Ukuri. ** Amanota ya 96% gusa bivuze ko icyitegererezo cyizeye cyane mubyiciro byacyo, ntabwo byanze bikunze ko umukoresha afite icyaha.

### 8. Kutamenya neza ubwabyo kuba ikimenyetso
Niba AI idashidikanywaho (urugero: Iterabwoba: 0,62, Satire: 0.54), ntishobora gufata ingamba zikarishye. Ahubwo, gushidikanya byubatswe muburyo bwububiko: ** Isubiramo ryabantu risabwa **.

### 9. Ibice bine byafashwe
- 🟢 ** GREEN **: Birashoboka cyane ko byujuje. → nta gikorwa.
- 🟡 ** UMUHondo **: Birashoboka kurenga. → kwitegereza / kwandika niba ari ngombwa.
- 🟠 ** ORANGE **: Birashoboka kurenga. Check Kugenzura.
- 🔴 ** UMUKARA **: Ihohoterwa rikomeye rishoboka. Measure igipimo cyo gukingira ako kanya + kwipimisha abantu.

### 10. Nta "Igihano cya AI"
** AI ntabwo itanga ibihano byanyuma. ** Irashobora gukurura ingamba za tekiniki zihutirwa (urugero nko guhagarika ubutumwa) mugihe habaye ibibazo bikomeye byumutekano, ariko icyemezo cya nyuma gikomeje kugenzurwa.

### 11. Ingamba zo gukingira zirashobora guhita
Niba hari iterabwoba ryihariye (Iterabwoba ryagaragaye → Icyizere cyinshi → Kubuza by'agateganyo → Gusubiramo abantu → Icyemezo), turinda umukoresha wugarijwe tutagize AI umucamanza.

### 12. AI igomba kuba ishobora kwemeza icyemezo cyayo
DSA isaba impamvu zisobanutse kandi zihariye. Impamvu za AI muburyo bwubatswe: Itegeko (NG-CONDUCT-004), Byagaragaye (Ibishobora gutera ubwoba), Icyizere (0.94), Imiterere ijyanye (Ubutumwa 4 bwambere), Igikorwa gisabwa (Isubiramo ryabantu).

### 13. AI ntabwo yemerewe guhindura rwihishwa ibirimo
** Kugereranya AI ntigomba na rimwe guhindura ibintu byumwimerere bitamenyekanye. ** Gukosora byikora, ibisobanuro cyangwa incamake bigumana ibintu byumwimerere.

### 14. AI yabyaye ibintu
Dutandukanya: ibyaremwe n'abantu, bifashwa na AI, byakozwe na AI na AI ikoreshwa. Ibi bihinduka mubice metadata.

### 15. Ibiranga AI Ibirango & AI Provenance Layeri
Ukurikije amategeko yo gukorera mu mucyo y’amategeko y’ubumwe bw’ibihugu by’Uburayi (kuva muri Kanama 2026), ibikomoka kuri AI bigomba kumenyekana. Turateganya urwego rwa AI Provenance Layeri ibika metadata (inkomoko ya AI, icyitegererezo, ingengabihe, isuzuma ryabantu).

### 16. Kumenya neza
Ubwubatsi bugamije kumenya amashusho yubukorikori, amajwi akangura hamwe nimbaraga zimbitse. Ariko kumenyekana ntabwo bihita bihamya.

### 17. Nta "mashini yukuri" yikora (kugereranya checking kugenzura ukuri)
Sisitemu igenzura: “Ibirimo birenga ku mategeko ayo ari yo yose?” (Ibirimo Ibirimo), undi aratanga: "Ni ayahe makuru n'inkomoko bihari?" (Gufasha Amakuru). Ibitekerezo ntibisibwa gusa nk "bibi".

### 18. Kurinda gusobanura nabi umuco
AI isaba ** Imiterere yimiterere yumuco ** kugirango ibuze ihame ryitumanaho ryigihugu kimwe kwemerwa nkurwego rwisi.

### 19. Igitangaje, urwenya no gusetsa
AI ikoresha imiterere, emojis, ibiganiro bitemba hamwe nuburyo buzwi bwicyuma, ariko bigomba kwemerera gushidikanya mugihe ibisobanuro bidasobanutse.

### 20. Nta gihano gishingiye ku manota imwe ya AI
Nta gikorwa gikomeye cyo kugereranya gishobora gushingira gusa kubisubizo bimwe byikora (Text + Context + Imyitwarire + Ururimi + Itangazamakuru + Imashini igenga = Isuzuma ry'ibyago).

### 21. Ibimenyetso by'imyitwarire y'abakoresha & Nta sisitemu y'inguzanyo
Byerekeranye nibimenyetso byo gukoresha tekiniki (urugero: kohereza ubutumwa bwa spam), ntabwo ari gahunda rusange yimibereho. Nexus Gaja ntabwo ikora sisitemu yinguzanyo - kugereranya ni umutekano kandi ntabwo isuzuma agaciro k'umuntu.

### 22. Kugereranya AI bigomba kugenzurwa
Ibyemezo byose byikora byanditse byanditse (indangamuntu y'ibyabaye, indangamuntu y'amategeko, ikizere, isubiramo ryabantu, nibindi) kugirango bikurikirane.

### 23. Ibyiza Byibinyoma, Ibibi Byibeshya & Ibipimo Byiza
Ubwoko bw'amakosa burakurikiranwa. Ikibaho gipima neza, kwibutsa na cyane cyane igipimo cya ** Kujurira ** (umubare wubujurire bwatsinze). 

### 24. Ururimi rwuburinganire & kubogama kubogamye
Ubwiza bugereranije bugomba kugereranywa mundimi zose zishyigikiwe (Indimi nyinshi zerekana urugero). Niba ibisubizo biciriritse bitandukanye hagati yumwimerere nubusobanuro (amakimbirane yubusobanuro), ibi bigomba kugenzurwa ukundi.

### 25. Ubwubatsi Icyifuzo & Politiki ya moteri
Amategeko (moteri ya politiki) ntabwo akomeye muburyo bwa AI. AI itanga ubushishozi, moteri ya politiki ifata ibyemezo bishingiye kumategeko ariho. Ibi bituma ** moderi ihinduka idahinduye amategeko **.

### 26. Abantu bakomeje kuba ubuyobozi bwa nyuma

Ubwubatsi butandukanya byimazeyo ibitekerezo kugirango tumenye neza amakuru kandi wirinde amakosa nka "ukoresha izina = umuntu":

- ** Imari: ** `Impano` ≠` Amafaranga asigaye. Ubwishyu bwoherejwe muburyo budasubirwaho mukigega ukoresheje igitabo (igitabo cyamafaranga).
